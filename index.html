What is Bring Your Own AI?

Bring Your Own AI is a native iOS chat client that lets you talk to every major AI model — OpenAI's GPT-4o, Anthropic's Claude, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama, Apple's on-device Foundation Model, and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — from a single, beautifully designed app.

You bring your own API keys. They are stored in the iOS Keychain with Secure Enclave-backed AES-256 encryption, never transmitted to any server, and never leave your device. We have no servers. There is nothing for us to collect, because nothing ever reaches us.

Why BYO-AI?

  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key). Your API keys live in the iOS Keychain, encrypted with hardware-backed AES-256. We never see them.
  • Every model, one app. Switch between GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, DeepSeek-V3, Llama 3, Qwen, your local Ollama, and Apple Intelligence without juggling browser tabs.
  • Thinking, visible. Reasoning models (DeepSeek-R1, o1, etc.) expose their chain-of-thought — collapse or expand on demand.
  • Streaming Markdown. Watch responses render token by token with full Markdown, LaTeX, and syntax-highlighted code blocks.
  • Local-first. Every conversation lives only on your device. AirDrop to back up.
